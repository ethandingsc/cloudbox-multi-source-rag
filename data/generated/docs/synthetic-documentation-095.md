# Team Accounts: Team Accounts and Roles — Quick reference

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on team accounts and roles. Business and Enterprise plans include team accounts with three roles. Admins manage membership, billing, and security settings. Members have full access to team folders according to their permissions. Guests are external people with limited access to specific folders only. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Team Accounts documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
