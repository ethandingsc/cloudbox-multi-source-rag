# Billing and Plans: Downgrading Your Plan — Expected behavior

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on downgrading your plan. Files above the new quota become read-only/download-only for 90 days, and new uploads are blocked. Shared links to over-quota content remain live during those 90 days but only allow viewing and downloading — no uploads through links. After 90 days, over-quota files are moved to the recycle bin and those shared links expire. To keep everything, either free up space or upgrade again within the 90-day window. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Billing and Plans documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
