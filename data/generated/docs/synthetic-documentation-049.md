# Mobile Apps: Mobile App Overview — Quick reference

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on mobile app overview. The CloudBox mobile app is available for iOS 15+ and Android 10+. It can browse, search, upload, and share your files, and it streams previews of documents, photos, and videos up to 500 MB directly in the app. Sign in with your CloudBox account; organization accounts follow the same 2FA requirement as the desktop clients. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Mobile Apps documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
