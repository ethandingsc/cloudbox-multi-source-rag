# Billing and Plans: Downgrading Your Plan — Common questions

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on downgrading your plan. Shared links to over-quota content remain live during those 90 days but only allow viewing and downloading — no uploads through links. After 90 days, over-quota files are moved to the recycle bin and those shared links expire. To keep everything, either free up space or upgrade again within the 90-day window. When you downgrade to a plan with less storage, CloudBox never deletes your data immediately. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Billing and Plans documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
