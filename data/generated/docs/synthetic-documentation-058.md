# Recycle Bin and Deletion: Retention Periods by Plan — Expected behavior

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on retention periods by plan. Once the retention window has passed, the files are gone — they cannot be recovered by you or by CloudBox support. If an item is missing from the recycle bin, either the retention window expired or someone emptied the bin. Deleted items stay in the recycle bin for 30 days on the free plan and 90 days on paid plans, after which they are permanently purged. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Recycle Bin and Deletion documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
