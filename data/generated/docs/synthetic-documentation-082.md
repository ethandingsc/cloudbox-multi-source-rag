# Storage Limits: Transfer and Bandwidth Limits — Expected behavior

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on transfer and bandwidth limits. Paid plans have no daily cap, subject to fair-use limits. Downloads of shared links by anonymous visitors count against the link owner's transfer limit. Desktop clients can throttle bandwidth per network in Settings > Network, which is useful on metered connections. Free plans include 200 GB per day of combined upload and download traffic. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Storage Limits documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
