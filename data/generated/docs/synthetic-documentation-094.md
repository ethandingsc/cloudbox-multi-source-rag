# Sync Engine: Local Cache and Disk Usage — Expected behavior

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on local cache and disk usage. You can clear it at any time under Settings > Advanced without affecting your files. The cache never counts against your CloudBox storage quota — it is a local, per-device artifact only. The client keeps a local cache of recently changed blocks to make syncs faster; this cache can grow large on busy folders. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Sync Engine documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
