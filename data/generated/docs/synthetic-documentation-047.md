# Getting Started: Your First Sync — Quick reference

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on your first sync. After signing in to a desktop client, CloudBox creates a local CloudBox folder. Anything you put inside it syncs to the cloud and to your other devices; anything you put in the cloud appears in the folder. The tray icon shows sync state: green when up to date, spinning blue while syncing, red on errors. Right-click the icon to pause, open preferences, or check sync activity. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Getting Started documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
