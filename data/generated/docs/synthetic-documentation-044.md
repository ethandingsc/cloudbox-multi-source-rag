# Getting Started: Creating an Account — Expected behavior

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on creating an account. Personal accounts can optionally enable two-factor authentication in the Security panel; organization accounts are required to enroll in 2FA by their administrator's policy (mandatory for all orgs since CloudBox 2.1). You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from the billing portal. Create a CloudBox account with your email address and a password of at least 12 characters, then verify your email address to activate it. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Getting Started documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
