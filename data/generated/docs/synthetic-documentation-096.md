# Team Accounts: Team Accounts and Roles — Expected behavior

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on team accounts and roles. Admins manage membership, billing, and security settings. Members have full access to team folders according to their permissions. Guests are external people with limited access to specific folders only. Every role is tied to a named user account; shared accounts are not supported. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Team Accounts documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
