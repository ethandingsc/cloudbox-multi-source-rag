# Billing and Plans: Invoices and Receipts — Common questions

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on invoices and receipts. Failed payments trigger a 14-day grace period before access is limited to view/download-only mode. Invoices are issued monthly from the billing portal at the start of each cycle. You can download PDF receipts for the last 24 months, change the billing email, and switch between credit card and bank transfer (Business and Enterprise). For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Billing and Plans documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
