# Team Accounts: Organization Security Requirements — Expected behavior

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on organization security requirements. Organizations on Business and Enterprise plans can additionally enable SSO (SAML or OIDC), which centralizes sign-in but does not replace the 2FA requirement. Admins can audit devices and sessions from the admin console and remotely sign out a lost laptop. All organization accounts must enroll in two-factor authentication; this requirement took effect with CloudBox 2.1 and applies to every member, including guests. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Team Accounts documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
