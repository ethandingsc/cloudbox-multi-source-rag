# Mobile Apps: Offline Access and Camera Upload — Expected behavior

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on offline access and camera upload. Camera upload sends new photos and videos from your device to the Camera Uploads folder automatically, in original quality. Camera uploads count against your storage quota and can be paused per network. Mark any file or folder as "Available offline" to pin a copy on the device; pinned items stay in sync over Wi-Fi by default. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Mobile Apps documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
