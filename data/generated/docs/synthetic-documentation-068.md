# Security: Recovery Codes and Account Lockout — Expected behavior

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on recovery codes and account lockout. If you lose both your authenticator and your codes, you must complete an identity verification with support, which takes up to 48 hours. Organization accounts additionally allow an administrator to reset a member's 2FA from the admin console. When you enroll in 2FA you receive 10 one-time recovery codes; store them somewhere safe, because they are the only way back in if you lose your authenticator. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Security documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
