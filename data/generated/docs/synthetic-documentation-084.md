# Storage Limits: Version History Quota Rules — Expected behavior

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on version history quota rules. Versions do not count against your storage quota, but the oldest versions are pruned automatically after the retention window. You can restore any previous version from the file's Version history panel in the web app. Version history stores previous states of your files for 30 days on the free plan and 180 days on paid plans. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Storage Limits documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
