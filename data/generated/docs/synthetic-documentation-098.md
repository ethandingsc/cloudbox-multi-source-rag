# Team Accounts: Shared Team Folders — Expected behavior

## Current documented behavior

This CloudBox 2.1 support note focuses on shared team folders. Access is granted per folder to members or groups. When someone leaves the organization, their personal files are transferred to an admin-designated account and their team folder access is revoked immediately. Team folders cannot be converted back to personal folders. Team folders live in a separate namespace from personal files and are owned by the organization, not by individuals. For the complete explanation and related topics, consult the maintained Team Accounts documentation. This note summarizes that source and does not introduce a separate product policy.
