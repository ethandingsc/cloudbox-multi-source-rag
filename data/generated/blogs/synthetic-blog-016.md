# Billing and Plans: Invoices and Receipts — Practical notes

## What the guide says

A practical way to approach invoices and receipts is to start with the current Billing and Plans guide. Invoices are issued monthly from the billing portal at the start of each cycle. You can download PDF receipts for the last 24 months, change the billing email, and switch between credit card and bank transfer (Business and Enterprise). Failed payments trigger a 14-day grace period before access is limited to view/download-only mode. When a forum answer or an old screenshot differs, check the maintained documentation before changing your setup. This note is a scenario-oriented summary of the same facts.
