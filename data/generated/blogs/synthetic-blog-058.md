# Recycle Bin and Deletion: Retention Periods by Plan — What to check

## What the guide says

A practical way to approach retention periods by plan is to start with the current Recycle Bin and Deletion guide. Once the retention window has passed, the files are gone — they cannot be recovered by you or by CloudBox support. If an item is missing from the recycle bin, either the retention window expired or someone emptied the bin. Deleted items stay in the recycle bin for 30 days on the free plan and 90 days on paid plans, after which they are permanently purged. When a forum answer or an old screenshot differs, check the maintained documentation before changing your setup. This note is a scenario-oriented summary of the same facts.
