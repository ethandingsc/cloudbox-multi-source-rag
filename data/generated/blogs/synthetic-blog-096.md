# Team Accounts: Team Accounts and Roles — What to check

## What the guide says

A practical way to approach team accounts and roles is to start with the current Team Accounts guide. Admins manage membership, billing, and security settings. Members have full access to team folders according to their permissions. Guests are external people with limited access to specific folders only. Every role is tied to a named user account; shared accounts are not supported. When a forum answer or an old screenshot differs, check the maintained documentation before changing your setup. This note is a scenario-oriented summary of the same facts.
