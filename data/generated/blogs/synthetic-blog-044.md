# Getting Started: Creating an Account — What to check

## What the guide says

A practical way to approach creating an account is to start with the current Getting Started guide. Personal accounts can optionally enable two-factor authentication in the Security panel; organization accounts are required to enroll in 2FA by their administrator's policy (mandatory for all orgs since CloudBox 2.1). You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from the billing portal. Create a CloudBox account with your email address and a password of at least 12 characters, then verify your email address to activate it. When a forum answer or an old screenshot differs, check the maintained documentation before changing your setup. This note is a scenario-oriented summary of the same facts.
