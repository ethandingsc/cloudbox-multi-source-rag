# Security: Recovery Codes and Account Lockout — Practical notes

## What the guide says

A practical way to approach recovery codes and account lockout is to start with the current Security guide. When you enroll in 2FA you receive 10 one-time recovery codes; store them somewhere safe, because they are the only way back in if you lose your authenticator. If you lose both your authenticator and your codes, you must complete an identity verification with support, which takes up to 48 hours. Organization accounts additionally allow an administrator to reset a member's 2FA from the admin console. When a forum answer or an old screenshot differs, check the maintained documentation before changing your setup. This note is a scenario-oriented summary of the same facts.
