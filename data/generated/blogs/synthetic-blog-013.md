# Billing and Plans: Downgrading Your Plan — Practical notes

## What the guide says

A practical way to approach downgrading your plan is to start with the current Billing and Plans guide. When you downgrade to a plan with less storage, CloudBox never deletes your data immediately. Files above the new quota become read-only/download-only for 90 days, and new uploads are blocked. Shared links to over-quota content remain live during those 90 days but only allow viewing and downloading — no uploads through links. After 90 days, over-quota files are moved to the recycle bin and those shared links expire. When a forum answer or an old screenshot differs, check the maintained documentation before changing your setup. This note is a scenario-oriented summary of the same facts.
