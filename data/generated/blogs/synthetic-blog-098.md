# Team Accounts: Shared Team Folders — What to check

## What the guide says

A practical way to approach shared team folders is to start with the current Team Accounts guide. Access is granted per folder to members or groups. When someone leaves the organization, their personal files are transferred to an admin-designated account and their team folder access is revoked immediately. Team folders cannot be converted back to personal folders. Team folders live in a separate namespace from personal files and are owned by the organization, not by individuals. When a forum answer or an old screenshot differs, check the maintained documentation before changing your setup. This note is a scenario-oriented summary of the same facts.
