# Storage Limits: Transfer and Bandwidth Limits — Practical notes

## What the guide says

A practical way to approach transfer and bandwidth limits is to start with the current Storage Limits guide. Free plans include 200 GB per day of combined upload and download traffic. Paid plans have no daily cap, subject to fair-use limits. Downloads of shared links by anonymous visitors count against the link owner's transfer limit. Desktop clients can throttle bandwidth per network in Settings > Network, which is useful on metered connections. When a forum answer or an old screenshot differs, check the maintained documentation before changing your setup. This note is a scenario-oriented summary of the same facts.
