# Billing and Plans: Downgrading Your Plan — A closer look

## What the guide says

A practical way to approach downgrading your plan is to start with the current Billing and Plans guide. Shared links to over-quota content remain live during those 90 days but only allow viewing and downloading — no uploads through links. After 90 days, over-quota files are moved to the recycle bin and those shared links expire. To keep everything, either free up space or upgrade again within the 90-day window. When you downgrade to a plan with less storage, CloudBox never deletes your data immediately.
