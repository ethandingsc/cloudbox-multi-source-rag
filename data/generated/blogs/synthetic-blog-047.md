# Getting Started: Your First Sync — Practical notes

## What the guide says

A practical way to approach your first sync is to start with the current Getting Started guide. After signing in to a desktop client, CloudBox creates a local CloudBox folder. Anything you put inside it syncs to the cloud and to your other devices; anything you put in the cloud appears in the folder. The tray icon shows sync state: green when up to date, spinning blue while syncing, red on errors. Right-click the icon to pause, open preferences, or check sync activity. When a forum answer or an old screenshot differs, check the maintained documentation before changing your setup. This note is a scenario-oriented summary of the same facts.
