# Mobile Apps: Offline Access and Camera Upload — Practical notes

## What the guide says

A practical way to approach offline access and camera upload is to start with the current Mobile Apps guide. Mark any file or folder as "Available offline" to pin a copy on the device; pinned items stay in sync over Wi-Fi by default. Camera upload sends new photos and videos from your device to the Camera Uploads folder automatically, in original quality. Camera uploads count against your storage quota and can be paused per network. When a forum answer or an old screenshot differs, check the maintained documentation before changing your setup. This note is a scenario-oriented summary of the same facts.
