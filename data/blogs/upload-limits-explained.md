# Why Is My Upload Capped at 5 GB?

## The 5 GB Ceiling

If you've tried to move a large video project into CloudBox, you've met the current single-file upload ceiling: 5 GB. The same limit applies to desktop clients, the web app, and API uploads. Attempts above it fail with the "File too large" error. This post reflects CloudBox 1.x behavior — check the official docs for the limits of the version you're actually running.

## Workarounds for Big Files

Until the limit moves, the practical options are: split large files into multi-part archives (under 5 GB each, obviously), keep raw footage on external drives and sync only deliverables, or use a different transfer method for the one-time big moves. Splitting is boring but reliable, and CloudBox handles archive files perfectly well.

## Looking Ahead

The good news: this is the most-requested change on the feature tracker, and we've heard loud and clear that video people need bigger single-file uploads. Watch the release notes later this year — let's just say the ceiling is not long for this world.
