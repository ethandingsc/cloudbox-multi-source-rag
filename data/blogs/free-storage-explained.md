# Getting the Most from Your Free 5 GB

## What the Free Plan Includes

The CloudBox free plan includes 5 GB of storage — enough for documents and photos, but it disappears fast once videos and archives enter the picture. The quota covers everything you own across synced folders and web uploads, and CloudBox warns you by email and in-app banner as you approach the limit. Note: this post describes the free plan as of CloudBox 1.0; limits may change in future versions.

## Watching Your Quota

The account page shows a quota bar with a breakdown of what's using space. Before upgrading, try these: clear out old file versions you don't need, empty the recycle bin, and unshare folders from other people that count against your quota. Photos from your phone add up surprisingly fast, so check camera upload settings.

## When to Upgrade

If you're bumping against 5 GB every month, upgrading to a paid plan adds terabytes of storage and longer version history. For occasional large transfers, remember the free tier's daily transfer allowance is generous enough for one-time moves — you don't have to stay subscribed to move a big archive once.
