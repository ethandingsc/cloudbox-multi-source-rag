# Mobile Apps

## Mobile App Overview

The CloudBox mobile app is available for iOS 15+ and Android 10+. It can browse, search, upload, and share your files, and it streams previews of documents, photos, and videos up to 500 MB directly in the app. Sign in with your CloudBox account; organization accounts follow the same 2FA requirement as the desktop clients.

## Offline Access and Camera Upload

Mark any file or folder as "Available offline" to pin a copy on the device; pinned items stay in sync over Wi-Fi by default. Camera upload sends new photos and videos from your device to the Camera Uploads folder automatically, in original quality. Camera uploads count against your storage quota and can be paused per network.

## Mobile Limitations

The mobile app does not support selective sync, bandwidth throttling, or LAN sync. Uploads from mobile clients support files up to 20 GB, subject to free-space checks on the device. Files stored offline are encrypted with the device's own disk encryption; the app adds no additional encryption layer.
