# Billing and Plans

## Plans and Pricing

CloudBox offers four plans. Free: 10 GB for individuals. Plus: 2 TB for $9.99 per month. Business: 10 TB shared across the organization with team folders, roles, and SSO, at $14.99 per user per month. Enterprise: custom storage and contract terms. Plan changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle; upgrades apply immediately with prorated charges.

## Downgrading Your Plan

When you downgrade to a plan with less storage, CloudBox never deletes your data immediately. Files above the new quota become **read-only/download-only for 90 days**, and new uploads are blocked. Shared links to over-quota content remain live during those 90 days but only allow viewing and downloading — no uploads through links. After 90 days, over-quota files are moved to the recycle bin and those shared links expire. To keep everything, either free up space or upgrade again within the 90-day window.

## Invoices and Receipts

Invoices are issued monthly from the billing portal at the start of each cycle. You can download PDF receipts for the last 24 months, change the billing email, and switch between credit card and bank transfer (Business and Enterprise). Failed payments trigger a 14-day grace period before access is limited to view/download-only mode.
