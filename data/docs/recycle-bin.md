# Recycle Bin and Deletion

## How the Recycle Bin Works

When you delete a file or folder in any CloudBox client, it moves to the recycle bin instead of being destroyed. Restoring an item returns it to its original location with its sharing settings intact. Items in the recycle bin do not count against your storage quota, but they remain visible to anyone who had access to the original item's folder.

## Retention Periods by Plan

Deleted items stay in the recycle bin for **30 days on the free plan** and **90 days on paid plans**, after which they are permanently purged. Once the retention window has passed, the files are gone — they cannot be recovered by you or by CloudBox support. If an item is missing from the recycle bin, either the retention window expired or someone emptied the bin.

## Permanent Deletion

Emptying the recycle bin, or deleting an item from inside the bin, is **irreversible**. There is no recovery path for permanently deleted files, including through support. Organization administrators can disable the "Empty recycle bin" button for members via the admin console if accidental mass deletions are a concern.
