# Getting Started

## Creating an Account

Create a CloudBox account with your email address and a password of at least 12 characters, then verify your email address to activate it. Personal accounts can optionally enable two-factor authentication in the Security panel; organization accounts are required to enroll in 2FA by their administrator's policy (mandatory for all orgs since CloudBox 2.1). You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from the billing portal.

## Installing CloudBox Clients

CloudBox runs on the web, on Windows 10+, macOS 11+, Linux (since version 2.0), iOS 15+, and Android 10+. Install the desktop client for background sync, or simply use the web app in any modern browser — no installation required. All clients sign in with the same account, and files appear everywhere automatically.

## Your First Sync

After signing in to a desktop client, CloudBox creates a local CloudBox folder. Anything you put inside it syncs to the cloud and to your other devices; anything you put in the cloud appears in the folder. The tray icon shows sync state: green when up to date, spinning blue while syncing, red on errors. Right-click the icon to pause, open preferences, or check sync activity.
